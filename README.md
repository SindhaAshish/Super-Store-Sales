# 🛒 Super Store Sales Analysis 

## 📊 Project Overview
This project involves an in-depth data analysis and visualization of the classic **Superstore Sales dataset**. The goal of this project is to analyze the historical sales data of a retail superstore to extract actionable business insights, understand customer behavior, and evaluate profitability across various product categories and geographical regions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
* **Sales & Profit Tracking:** Monitor revenue and profit margins across different time periods (Year-over-Year and Month-over-Month).
* **Category Analysis:** Identify the most and least profitable product categories and sub-categories.
* **Geographical Insights:** Determine which regions, states, or cities generate the highest sales.
* **Customer Segmentation:** Analyze consumer behavior and segment sales by customer types (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office).

## 📁 Dataset Details
The dataset contains transactional records of a superstore, including:
* **Order Details:** `Order ID`, `Order Date`, `Ship Date`, `Ship Mode`
* **Customer Information:** `Customer ID`, `Customer Name`, `Segment`
* **Geography:** `Country`, `City`, `State`, `Postal Code`, `Region`
* **Product Details:** `Product ID`, `Category`, `Sub-Category`, `Product Name`
* **Financial Metrics:** `Sales`, `Quantity`, `Discount`, `Profit`

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Analyzed
1. **Total Sales:** Overall revenue generated by the superstore.
2. **Total Profit:** Net profit calculated after discounts and costs.
3. **Average Order Value (AOV):** The average amount spent per order.
4. **Profit Margin:** The percentage of revenue that translates into profit.
5. **Sales by Region/Category:** Breakdown of performance by geographical and product dimensions.
